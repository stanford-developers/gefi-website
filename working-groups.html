Working Groups

Working groups bring GEFI members together to exchange ideas and work on shared research questions, resources, and activities.

Propose a working group

Have an idea for a collaboration, resource, discussion group, or event? GEFI members can propose a working group with at least three members, including a lead, and a one-paragraph charter describing its purpose, planned activities or outputs, and approximate timeline.

The Steering Committee reviews proposals during its quarterly meetings or through asynchronous discussion. The review timeline will depend on the proposal and committee availability.
