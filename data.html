Working with the data

  1. Choose a study

    Check the setting, participants, measures, and collection period. Instruments and variable names can differ across studies.

  2. Read its documentation

    Use the study’s codebook, response scales, and adaptation notes. A shared instrument name does not by itself establish comparable scores across contexts.

  3. Follow the access and use terms

    When Redivis links are available, each dataset will explain whether access is open or requires approval. Follow its license and citation instructions.
